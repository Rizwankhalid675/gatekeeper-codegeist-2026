And the audit trail is "the model said so". A service desk does not have a detection problem, it has a volume problem — but the obvious fix, letting a language model read the queue and act on it, is unacceptable the moment the queue belongs to someone else’s production system.

How it works

A model proposes. Code decides.

Three trust levels. Correlation is deterministic and fully trusted: it decides what deserves attention at all, before a model is asked anything. The two Rovo agents are untrusted and load-bearing — delete them and there is no diagnosis, but they are never the authority. The policy gate and executor are deterministic and fully trusted: eleven rules decide whether a robot may act, and the executor re-derives permission from the rule pack rather than trusting the verdict it was handed.
